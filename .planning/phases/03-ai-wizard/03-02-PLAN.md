wizard Pinia store with the polling loop, the four step components, the stepper, the widget,
the page, and the `/ai-wizard` route.

Storage-upload fallback documented there applies and `invokeGenerateQuiz` / step 2 are
revisited — until then, base64-in-JSON is the locked transport.
